# 5.1 System Quality

> **Status: placeholder.**

To be written: the continuous analysis performed on the source, the measurements it produces, and the outcome against the criteria adopted in [4.5](../implementation/quality-criteria).

Intended scope:

- **The analysis** — what is submitted, by which workflow, and on which events. What the analysis examines that static analysis of [4.2](../implementation/coding-standards) does not.
- **Measurements reported** — reliability, security, maintainability, coverage and duplication, and what each counts.
- **Outcome against the adopted criteria** — the state of the quality gate, and the value measured for each of the two thresholds.
- **Issues raised and their resolution** — what the analysis reported, how each was classified, and what was done about it. The four recorded states of the project, before and after resolution.
- **Security hotspots** — those reviewed, and the decision recorded for each.
- **What the analysis does not establish** — the limits of static measurement, and the reason behaviour is examined separately in [5.2](./system-testing).

The measurements are of the source in aggregate as well as of new code. The criteria of [4.5](../implementation/quality-criteria) are applied to new code, and the aggregate figures are reported here as context rather than as conditions.
