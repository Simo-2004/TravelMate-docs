# 4. Saving Trips and Companions

This chapter covers saving a trip or a companion so you can return to it, and finding or removing what you saved.

## Saving an item

Open a trip or a companion and tap the **bookmark button** on the right. It is below the images on the itinerary screen for a trip, and beside the name for a companion.

Two things confirm it worked: the button turns yellow, and a short message appears at the bottom of the screen.

The button stays yellow whenever you return to that trip or companion, so a glance tells you whether it is already saved.

| Action | Message |
|--------|---------|
| Trip saved | *Trip saved to Saved Items.* |
| Trip removed | *Trip removed from Saved Items.* |
| Companion saved | *Mate profile saved to Saved Items.* |
| Companion removed | *Mate profile removed from Saved Items.* |

## The Saved tab

Everything you saved is in the **Saved** tab. Trips and companions are held in one list rather than kept apart, with the most recently saved at the top.

Each card shows the name, the description and the tags. A companion is marked with **Profile:** before the name, which is what tells the two kinds apart at a glance.

Tapping a card opens the same screen you saved it from — the itinerary for a trip, the profile for a companion.

## Removing an item

Tap the bookmark button again, either on the item itself or after opening it from the Saved tab. The button loses its colour, a message confirms the removal, and the card leaves the tab.

There is no confirmation step, but nothing is lost by removing something: saving it again puts it back.

## What is kept

Saved items stay on the phone. Closing the app, or signing out and back in, leaves them as they were.

Saving a trip is also what makes it available to share in a conversation, covered in [chapter 5](./conversations).
