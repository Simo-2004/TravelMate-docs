# 6. Your Profile

> **Status: placeholder.**

To be written: what your profile holds, how to change each part of it, and how it is stored.

Intended scope:

- **Where the profile is** — reached from the Settings tab.
- **What it holds** — photo, name and surname, description, interests, and the kinds of trip you prefer.
- **Making changes** — the control that turns the screen into an editable form.
- **Changing the photo** — choosing an image from the phone, and what happens if the choice is cancelled or fails.
- **Interests and trip tags** — adding a tag, removing one, and why a tag already in the list is not added twice.
- **Saving or discarding** — saving keeps the changes, leaving without saving discards them and nothing is kept in the meantime.
- **How the profile is stored** — kept on the device in protected form, readable only by the app on that phone.
