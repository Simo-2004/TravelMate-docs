# 6. Your Profile

This chapter covers what your profile holds and how to change it.

## Opening your profile

Go to the **Settings** tab and tap your profile at the top.

The screen holds your photo, your name, a description, and two sets of tags: **Personal interests** and **Personal trip tags**. The tags are what companions and trips are matched against, so they are worth keeping current.

## Making changes

Tap **Edit profile**. The screen becomes a form with everything editable.

The form is longer than the screen, so scroll down to reach the buttons at the end.

| Field | Rules |
|-------|-------|
| **Name** | Required, up to 40 characters |
| **Surname** | Required, up to 40 characters |
| **Description** | Optional, up to 300 characters |

## Changing the photo

Under **Photo** there are two ways to set a picture:

- **Pick one of the ready-made pictures** in the row. The selected one is outlined in yellow.
- **Tap Upload photo** to use a picture from the phone. It is copied into the app, so deleting the original from the gallery later does not remove it from your profile.

If you close the gallery without choosing, nothing changes. If the picture cannot be read, the app says so and the form stays as it was.

## Changing tags

Each set of tags has a field and an **Add personal tag** button. Type a tag and tap the button to add it.

To remove a tag, tap the **×** beside it. Tags only show this × while you are editing.

The same tag is not added twice, and capital letters do not make two tags different — adding one that is already there does nothing.

## Saving or discarding

Two buttons close the form:

- **Save profile changes** keeps everything you changed and returns to the profile.
- **Cancel** discards it all and returns the profile to how it was.

Nothing is saved while you are typing, so leaving the screen without saving also discards the changes. Once saved, the new details are used everywhere at once.

## Where the profile is kept

Your profile stays on the phone, in protected form. Someone who copies the app's files off the device cannot read your name, description or tags.

The photo is held as a file in the app's own storage rather than in the picture gallery.
