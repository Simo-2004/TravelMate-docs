# User Manual

> **Status: skeleton.** The structure is fixed; the chapters are placeholders and their content is to be written.

This manual explains how to use TravelMate. It covers opening the app for the first time, finding your way around it, and every task it lets you carry out.

It is written for someone using the app, not for someone building it. No technical knowledge is needed, and nothing here assumes you have read any other document on this site.

## Before you start

TravelMate runs on your phone and keeps everything on it. There is no account on a server, nothing is uploaded, and the app works with no internet connection.

This means two things worth knowing from the start:

- Your profile, your saved items and your conversations stay on the device you installed the app on.
- If you remove the app, that data is removed with it.

## How to use this manual

Each chapter covers one part of the app. If you are opening TravelMate for the first time, start at [1. Getting Started](./getting-started) and read on; otherwise go straight to the chapter for the task you want.
