# 2. Moving Around the App

This chapter covers the layout of the app and the four places the bar at the bottom leads to.

## The main screen

Every screen has the same three parts: the title at the top, the content in the middle, and the bar at the bottom.

The title always names the tab you are on. The selected tab in the bar is marked with a white circle behind its icon.

## The four tabs

| Tab | Holds |
|-----|-------|
| **Home** | Trips suggested to you and the ones you opened recently |
| **Search** | Searching for trips and companions ([chapter 3](./search)) |
| **Saved** | Everything you saved ([chapter 4](./saved)) |
| **Settings** | Your profile, privacy choices and help ([chapter 7](./settings)) |

Tap a tab to switch to it. Switching keeps nothing from the previous tab open, and switching back returns it as it was.

## The Home tab

Home holds a search field and two rows of trips.

- **Search on TravelMate** — tapping this field switches to the Search tab and puts the cursor in its field, so you can start typing at once. It is a shortcut, not a second search.
- **Recommended trips for you** — trips the app suggests.
- **Viewed recently** — trips you opened before.

Both rows scroll sideways. Tapping a trip in either row opens its itinerary.

## Opening and closing a screen

A screen opened from a tab — a trip, a companion, a conversation — sits on top of that tab rather than replacing it. Use the back gesture, or the arrow at the top left, to close it and return to where you were.

The bar at the bottom is not shown on these screens, because they belong to the tab underneath rather than being a tab of their own.

## While typing

The bar at the bottom is hidden whenever the keyboard is open, so the field you are filling stays visible. It comes back as soon as the keyboard closes.
