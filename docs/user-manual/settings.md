# 7. Settings and Privacy

This chapter covers the Settings tab, the privacy switches, where to find help, and signing out.

## Privacy

The Privacy screen holds four switches.

<div class="phone-shots">
  <figure>
    <img src="/static/manual/chapter7-image2.jpg" alt="The privacy switches" />
    <figcaption>The four privacy switches, all off</figcaption>
  </figure>
</div>

| Switch | Intended for |
|--------|-------------|
| **Private Profile** | Keeping your profile from being seen by others |
| **Only people in your radius** | Limiting who reaches you to those nearby |
| **Check messages** | Screening messages before they arrive |
| **Offline mode** | Appearing unavailable |

Tapping a switch confirms the change with a short message — *Private Profile On*, for example — and the setting is kept: closing the app and opening it again leaves the switches as you left them.

**These switches do not change anything yet.** The screen says so itself: they are prepared for a later version. Each of them describes something that only matters once the app can reach other people over a network, and this version keeps everything on your phone, so there is nothing for them to control. Turning one on records your choice and nothing more.

## Signing out

**Exit** returns the app to the sign-in screen and locks it. The back gesture does not bring you back in — the only way is to sign in again.

Signing out keeps everything: your profile, your saved items and your conversations are all there when you sign back in. It locks the app rather than clearing it.

## Removing your data

There is no button that erases everything. Removing the app from the phone is what removes its data, and **this cannot be undone** — nothing is stored anywhere else to restore from.

Individual things can be removed without going that far: un-save an item from the Saved tab ([chapter 4](./saved)), or clear a conversation from its own screen ([chapter 5](./conversations)).
