# 7. Settings and Privacy

> **Status: placeholder.**

To be written: what the Settings tab offers, what each privacy choice does, where to find help, and how to sign out.

Intended scope:

- **The Settings tab** — your profile at the top, and the actions below it.
- **Privacy preferences** — each switch, what it controls, and that a change takes effect at once and is remembered.
- **Help and support** — the questions answered in the app and how to get in touch.
- **Signing out** — what it does: the app returns to the sign-in screen and cannot be re-entered with the back gesture.
- **What signing out does not do** — your profile, saved items and conversations are kept, and signing in again brings them back.
- **Removing your data** — the only way to remove everything is to uninstall the app, which cannot be undone.
