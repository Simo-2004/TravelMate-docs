# 5. Conversations

> **Status: placeholder.**

To be written: how to start a conversation with a companion, send messages, share a trip with them, and clear a conversation.

Intended scope:

- **Starting a conversation** — the button on a companion's profile that opens the chat.
- **Sending a message** — the field at the bottom, and where your message appears.
- **Receiving a reply** — a reply arrives shortly after you send, and how it is shown apart from yours.
- **Whether someone is available** — the indicator at the top, and what makes it change.
- **Sharing a trip** — attaching a trip to a message, how the attached trip appears, and that tapping it opens the itinerary.
- **How a companion answers a shared trip** — the reply depends on whether the trip matches their interests.
- **Clearing a conversation** — how to empty it, and that this cannot be undone.
- **What is kept** — a conversation stays on the device and is still there when you reopen the app.
