# 5. Conversations

This chapter covers messaging a companion, sharing a trip with them, and clearing a conversation.

## Starting a conversation

Open a companion from the Search tab and tap **Chat** at the bottom of their profile. The conversation opens, empty the first time.

## Sending a message

Type in the field at the bottom and tap the arrow. Your message appears on the right in yellow, with the time it was sent. An empty message is not sent.

A reply arrives about a second later and appears on the left. Replies are chosen from what you wrote, so a greeting and a question about a trip are answered differently.

## What a companion replies to

A companion answers by looking for certain words in your message. The table below lists every word that gets a reply of its own, and the reply it produces.

| If your message contains | The reply is |
|--------------------------|--------------|
| how are you, how r u | *I'm doing great, thanks for asking! How about you?* |
| hello, hi, hey, yo | *Hey! Great to hear from you.* |
| trip, travel, destination, vacation, itinerary | *I'd love to plan our next trip together, tell me more!* |
| thank, thanks | *You're very welcome!* |
| name | *Nice to meet you!* |
| haha, lol, lmao, funny | *Haha, that's really funny!* |
| where | *I'm still deciding, but I'm open to suggestions!* |
| weather | *The weather looks great for traveling this week!* |
| food, eat, restaurant | *I'm always up for trying new food!* |
| photo, picture, pic | *I'll share some photos from my last trip soon!* |
| yes, sure, agree, ok, okay | *Sounds good to me!* |
| no, nah, not really | *No worries, that's fine.* |
| bye, goodbye, see you, later | *Talk to you soon, safe travels!* |
| anything else | *I don't understand this text, please try again* |

### How a message is matched

Three rules decide which reply you get, and knowing them makes the conversation predictable.

**Whole words only.** A word counts only when it stands on its own. Writing *trip* gets the travel reply; writing *triple* does not, because *trip* is only part of a longer word. Capital letters make no difference, so *Hi* and *hi* are the same.

**The list is read from the top, and the first match wins.** A message containing more than one of these words gets the reply for whichever appears **higher in the table**. Writing *hi, any trip ideas?* returns the greeting, not the travel reply, because greetings sit above trips in the list. To get the travel reply, leave the greeting out and write *any trip ideas?* on its own.

**Anything else gets the fallback.** A message with none of these words — and an empty one — returns *I don't understand this text, please try again*.

A conversation therefore works best one subject at a time: greet first, wait for the reply, then send the next message with only the word for the subject you want. Sharing a trip does not follow these rules at all, and is covered below.

## Whether a companion is available

Under the name at the top is **Online** with a green dot, or **Offline** with a grey one.

A companion goes online as soon as you send a message, and returns offline after a few seconds without further activity. It reflects the exchange you are having rather than whether the person is really at their phone.

## Sharing a trip

Tap the **+** button beside the message field. A row of your saved trips appears above it; tap one to share it.

The trip is sent as a card inside your message, with the question *Do you want to do this travel with me?* Tapping the card opens the itinerary.

Only trips you have saved can be shared. If you have saved none, the app says so and asks you to bookmark a trip first — see [chapter 4](./saved).

### How a companion answers

The reply depends on the trip, not on chance. The companion accepts when one of the trip's tags matches their interests or preferred trips, and refuses when none does.

| Outcome | Reply |
|---------|-------|
| Accepted | *I'd love to! This trip looks perfect for me, let's plan it together!* |
| Refused | *Thanks for the invite, but this trip isn't really my style. Maybe another one?* |

Their tags are shown on their profile, so you can tell before sharing which trips are likely to suit them.

## Clearing a conversation

The bin icon at the top right empties the conversation. It clears at once, with no confirmation step, and **the messages cannot be recovered**.

Clearing one conversation leaves the others untouched.

## What is kept

A conversation stays on the phone until you clear it. Leaving the screen, closing the app, or signing out and back in all leave it as it was.
